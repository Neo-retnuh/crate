# Perspective Sets Library

Pre-built lens combinations for common decision types.
Use these instead of auto-decompose when the decision type is clear.

## Decision Stress Test
- **Advocate**: Strongest case FOR. Lead with upside, tailwinds, why this is the right move.
- **Skeptic**: Strongest case AGAINST. Lead with failure modes, hidden costs, what breaks.
- **Pragmatist**: Implementation reality. Lead with what actually happens when you try to execute.

## Business Evaluation
- **Financial**: Revenue impact, cost structure, ROI, runway implications, cash flow.
- **Operational**: Can we actually execute this? Team capacity, dependencies, timeline reality.
- **Strategic**: Does this compound? Market positioning, competitive moat, 3-year trajectory.
- **Competitive**: How will competitors respond? What does this signal to the market?

## Content / Audience Review
- **Target Customer**: Does this speak to me? Would I take the CTA? Does the hook earn the read?
- **Competitor**: What does this reveal about positioning? Where are they vulnerable?
- **Internal Team**: Can I execute on what this promises? Is this aligned with what we actually deliver?
- **Casual Browser**: Would I stop scrolling? Do I understand what this is in 5 seconds?

## Pricing / Packaging
- **Buyer**: Is this worth the price? What am I comparing it to? What would make me say yes faster?
- **Seller**: Does this margin work? Can we deliver at this price sustainably?
- **Competitor**: How does this position against alternatives? Where does it leave us vulnerable?

## Architecture / Technical
- **Builder**: Can I actually build this? What's the maintenance burden? What breaks at scale?
- **User**: Does this make my life easier? Is the complexity justified by the outcome?
- **Operator**: Can I run this in production? What's the failure mode? How do I debug it?

## Hiring / People
- **Candidate**: Would I want this role? Is this a place I'd grow?
- **Team**: Does this person fill a real gap? Will they mesh with how we work?
- **Business**: Does this hire unlock revenue or reduce risk? What's the cost of not hiring?

## Research Synthesis
- **Themes**: What patterns emerge across sources? What's the dominant narrative?
- **Contradictions**: Where do sources disagree? Which contradictions matter?
- **Gaps**: What's NOT being said? What evidence is missing?
- **Implications**: So what? What should we do differently based on this?
